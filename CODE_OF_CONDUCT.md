# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free
environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.
- Use clear, inclusive language.
- Respect privacy and never publish real personal data.
- Report mistakes and help correct them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing, publishing private information or any other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or restrict participation when
behavior violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to the
maintainers through the repository's security or contact channel.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
